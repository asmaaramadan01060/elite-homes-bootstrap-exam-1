Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about buying, selling, and investing in real estate

A general rule of thumb is that your monthly housing costs shouldn't exceed 28% of your gross monthly income. We recommend getting pre-approved with one of our trusted mortgage lenders to determine your exact budget based on your income, debts, credit score, and down payment.

On average, the home buying process takes 30 to 45 days from the time your offer is accepted to closing day. However, searching for the right home can take anywhere from a few weeks to several months depending on market conditions and your specific criteria.

Closing costs typically range from 2% to 5% of the home's purchase price and include loan origination fees, appraisal fees, title insurance, property taxes, and homeowner's insurance. Your lender will provide a detailed Loan Estimate outlining all expected costs upfront.

This depends on your financial situation and local market conditions. We can help you explore options such as bridge loans, home sale contingencies, or rent-back agreements so you can transition smoothly without feeling rushed or carrying two mortgages.

A certified home inspector will evaluate the roof, foundation, electrical systems, plumbing, HVAC, insulation, and appliances. We always recommend attending the inspection so you can ask questions directly and understand any maintenance needs or potential repairs.

An experienced real estate agent provides local market expertise, access to off-market listings, skilled price negotiation, contract management, and guidance through inspections and closing—saving you time, stress, and potentially thousands of dollars.
